⚡ Key Architecture Principles

🔒 100% Offline & Private

No mandatory cloud logins, subscription fees, or remote server dependencies. Video feeds stream strictly over local RTSP & V4L2 networks.

🖥️ Multi-Monitor Pop-Outs

Pop camera feeds into independent floating windows. Drag windows over each other to automatically group them into multi-camera grid windows.

🔋 Low-Power Hardware

Optimized for Linux laptops (Intel N150, Pentium Gold 6500Y), OpenWrt routers, and mobile devices without requiring dedicated GPUs.
